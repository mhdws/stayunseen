Basics Is Stay Unseen free?

Yes. It's published to the Chrome, Edge and Firefox stores like any other extension, and installing it is the whole cost. There is no account, no trial, and nothing to unlock.

Compatibility Does it work in the Instagram or Facebook mobile app?

No. Stay Unseen is a browser extension, so it works on instagram.com, facebook.com and messenger.com in Chrome, Edge and Firefox. It cannot reach the native iOS or Android apps.

Trust Will it get my account blocked or banned?

Stay Unseen does not log in for you, automate anything, or scrape your account. It filters specific outgoing reports inside your own browser — the same category of tool as a content blocker — and it never adds anything to what you send.

Performance Does it slow the sites down?

No. It cancels those requests before they are made, so there is slightly less network work than usual, not more. The panel and its counters are local, so there is nothing extra to fetch.

Basics Do I have to configure anything?

No. All six blockers are on the moment it installs. The panel's seven switches exist to opt out of one signal on one site — not to set anything up.

Privacy What does it store, and does any of it leave my machine?

It keeps your switches, a blocked count per feature, and a short local log with message text removed. None of it is ever sent anywhere — there is no server — and uninstalling deletes all of it. The privacy policy lists every field.

Honesty Does it hide my online or active status?

No. It hides story and reel views, read receipts and typing indicators. Your active indicator is a different thing and is left untouched — see what it cannot do.
