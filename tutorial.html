Stay Unseen 1.1.0 · Chrome, Edge and Firefox

Install it, pin the icon, reload the tab — then just use Instagram, Facebook and Messenger normally. All six blockers ship on, so there is nothing to configure. Watch the walkthrough below.

The three steps

  • Add it from your browser's own store — confirm the prompt, three sites, nothing else.
  • Pin the icon so the counters are one click away on every tab.
  • Reload the tab so the page patch installs at document start.
